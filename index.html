TD Parlay Builder

Our chance blends the model (red-zone and goal-line opportunity, team totals from the spread and total) with the books' own prices. Players where the two disagree by 2x or more are left out: that gap usually means news the model can't see.

Legs in different games multiply cleanly. Teammates score together 8% less often than their odds imply and opponents 3% more, so same-game slips show our fair price; the app's own same-game price may differ.

Long parlays lose money on average. Size them like lottery tickets. Gambling problem? Call 1-800-GAMBLER.
